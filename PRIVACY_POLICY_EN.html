Privacy Policy

App: RouteRisk Weather / Weather risk on your route
Document version: 2026-10-04
Hrvatska verzija (Croatian)

This app helps estimate weather risk along a route or at a single location. The app does not require user registration and does not use its own user account system.

Data stored locally

The app may store the following data locally on the device:

  • saved routes and plans,
  • selected activity module,
  • trip start date or work-planning start date,
  • saved work locations,
  • manually entered arrival times,
  • trip journal: notes, ratings and photos the user adds to a day journal,
  • the last downloaded forecast and map images, for use without an internet connection,
  • basic app settings.

These data remain in the app's local storage on the device. Deleting the app also deletes the locally stored app data.

Data sent to external services

The app uses public external services:

  • Open-Meteo — coordinates and dates are sent to retrieve weather forecasts or historical weather data.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding / GeoNames — location search text is sent when searching for places.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — used as a fallback location search provider; location search text is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap tile server — map tiles are loaded for the area viewed by the user.
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) — only when the user asks for lodging near an overnight stop; the coordinates of that place are sent.

The app does not send saved routes to the app authors and does not use its own server to store these data.

Photos and on-device text recognition

When importing a list of places, the user may pick an existing image from the gallery or take a photo of a written list. The text is read from the image entirely on the device (offline text recognition). The image is not sent to the app author or to any external service, is not stored outside the device, and is used only to recognise place names at that moment. The app never accesses the gallery on its own — the user selects the image.

Trip journal

A day journal (notes, ratings, arrival time and photos the user picks from the gallery) is stored only on the device. Photos are copied into the app's private folder, resized and without hidden data (EXIF, including the location where they were taken). None of it is sent to the app author or to external services; a day report or travelogue is sent to others only by the user, and only when the user chooses to.

Plan export and sharing

A plan, a day report and a travelogue can be exported and shared through the Android system share sheet. The user chooses the recipient and the sending app; the app never sends anything on its own and keeps no copy of the report outside the device.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The current version does not use Firebase Analytics, AdMob, advertising networks, or custom remote analytics. The app does not collect personal data for marketing tracking.

Accuracy notice

Weather forecasts and statistical estimates are planning aids. The app is not an official meteorological source and cannot replace official warnings, GPS navigation, or assessment of real-world conditions.
